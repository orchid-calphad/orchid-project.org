# ORCHID logo — specification v1.0 (2026-10-22)

The mark is a **spinodal rose with an orchid in the stable centre**: a ternary (Gibbs) triangle in which three
spinodal lobes (λ₁ = 0, after Morral & Chen's stability maps) sit on the three binary edges, each with a critical
point, and a stylised moth orchid (Phalaenopsis) occupies the stable island in the middle. It reads as a flower and
is a thermodynamically meaningful schematic: unstable regions near each binary miscibility gap, a stable centre, stable
corners.

## 1. Coordinate system

- SVG user units; master artboard `viewBox="0 -2 186 172"` (width 186, height 172).
- Origin top-left, y downward. All rotations are about the **pivot P = (93, 106)**, the centroid of the triangle.

Approximate CMYK for print (coated): Orchid 52/80/0/0, Ink 0/0/0/95. Pantone nearest: Orchid ≈ 2593 C.

**On dark backgrounds** (≥ #2A2733): triangle and labels → #FFFFFF; contours → #B9AEC6; lobe fill opacity 0.28;
everything else unchanged. **One-colour** (embossing, fax, single-ink print): every element in Ink (or white),
lobe fills removed, petals as outline only, column omitted.

## 4. Typography

- **Wordmark:** "ORCHID", all caps, **IBM Plex Sans SemiBold (600)**, size = 0.337 × mark height (58 px for a 172 px
  mark), tracking +0.03 em (letter-spacing 1.74 px at 58 px), no kerning overrides.
- **Tagline:** "Open Repository for CALPHAD Hypotheses, Intelligence, and Discovery", **IBM Plex Sans Regular (400)**,
  14 px at the master size (0.0814 × mark height), #5E5A66, line-height 1.35, broken after "Hypotheses,".
- **Diagram labels:** **IBM Plex Serif Italic**, 12 px at master size.
- Fonts: IBM Plex (SIL Open Font License 1.1), https://github.com/IBM/plex — free for any use. For production files,
  convert text to outlines.

## 5. Lockups

- **Horizontal (primary):** `orchid-lockup.svg`, 544 × 172. Mark at x 0–186; wordmark baseline-left at (214, 92);
  tagline lines at (214, 120) and (214, 139). Gap mark→text = 28 px (0.163 × mark height).
- **Stacked:** mark centred above wordmark; gap 0.15 × mark height; tagline optional.
- **Mark only:** `orchid-mark.svg`.
- **App icon / favicon:** `orchid-icon.svg`, 96 × 96 tile, corner radius 20, fill Ink; artwork = lobes as solid
  #7B3FA0 (no contours, no critical points), triangle stroke white 7 (in mark units), orchid in white (sepals 85%
  opacity) with the lip in #7B3FA0, column omitted; artwork box 80 × 74 at (8, 11) showing mark units (4, 4)–(182, 160).

## 6. Clear space and minimum size

- Clear space on all sides = height of the critical-point ring × 4 = **0.1 × mark height** (17 px at 172 px).
- Minimum sizes: full mark with labels and contours ≥ 64 px tall; without labels ≥ 40 px; below 40 px use the app icon
  (legible to 16 px as a favicon).

## 7. Don'ts

Don't recolour the lobes and orchid differently from each other; don't rotate the triangle (A stays on top); don't
add gradients, glows or shadows; don't stretch; don't redraw the orchid larger than the stable island without
pulling the lobes back proportionally; don't set the wordmark in another typeface.

## 8. Generation prompt (for designers or image models)

> A flat, vector scientific logo for "ORCHID" (Open Repository for CALPHAD Hypotheses, Intelligence, and Discovery).
> An equilateral triangle outlined in near-black (#1D1B20), point up, corners labelled A (top), B (bottom left),
> C (bottom right) in a small italic serif. On each of the three edges sits a shallow dome-shaped region, lightly
> tinted violet (#7B3FA0 at 16% opacity), bounded by a thicker violet curve that bulges toward the centre; at the
> innermost point of each curve is a small solid violet dot with a thin white ring. Inside each dome, three thin
> sage-green (#9BB8A4) curves run parallel to the violet curve, getting smaller toward the edge, like contour lines;
> one more sage curve wraps around each corner of the triangle between domes. In the empty centre sits a small,
> stylised moth orchid (Phalaenopsis) seen from the front: one narrow upper sepal and two narrow lower sepals in
> semi-transparent violet, two broad pale-lavender (#F3EAF8) petals with thin violet outlines, a small violet lip
> below and a tiny golden (#F2C94C) column in the middle. To the right, the word "ORCHID" in IBM Plex Sans SemiBold,
> uppercase, slightly letter-spaced, near-black, with the tagline beneath in IBM Plex Sans Regular, grey (#5E5A66).
> White background, crisp, no gradients, no shadows, generous margins. The image is a ternary phase-diagram stability
> map (spinodal lobes with critical points around a stable centre) that doubles as a flower.
