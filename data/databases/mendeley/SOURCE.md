# Mendeley Data CALPHAD databases

This source family contains eight CALPHAD databases published as versioned
Mendeley Data datasets under CC BY 4.0 or CC0 1.0. Six remain bundled in this
directory. `BITE.TDB` and `TDB_Fe-Mg.TDB` are retained as source-fetched catalog
records after the SGTE binary consolidation; their historical hashes preserve
the identity of the formerly bundled bytes. `Mg-Sc-Zn.txt` and `Ti-M.TDB.txt`
were renamed only to give them a usable `.tdb` extension.
